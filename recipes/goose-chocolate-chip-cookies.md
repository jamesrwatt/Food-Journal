# Goose's Chocolate Chip Cookies

**Oven:** Preheat to 350°F | **Bake Time:** 10-12 minutes

| Ingredients | Instructions |
|---|---|
| 1 cup salted butter | 1. Whisk together flour, baking soda, and salt. |
| 1 1/4 cups brown sugar | 2. In a pan over medium heat, add the butter. Cook 5-6 minutes until it starts to bubble, then browns. Transfer to a bowl and allow to cool. |
| 1 1/3 cups granulated sugar | 3. In a large mixing bowl, add brown sugar and granulated sugar, then pour in the butter. Mix until combined. Add the egg, egg yolks, and vanilla. Whisk until smooth. |
| 1 large egg + 2 egg yolks (room temp) | 4. Add wet ingredients into dry ingredients and mix to form dough. |
| 1 tablespoon vanilla | 5. Mix in chocolate chips and let dough rest 15-20 minutes. |
| 2 cups all-purpose flour | 6. Line a sheet pan with parchment paper. |
| 3/4 tsp baking soda | 7. Scoop dough into portions about 1/4 to 1/3 cup each. Space them out, about 6 cookies per sheet. |
| 1/2 tsp salt | 8. Bake about 10 minutes until edges are golden brown and centers are still a bit soft. Cookies will continue to bake on the pan once out of the oven. |
| 1 cup semi-sweet chocolate chips | |
| 1 cup dark chocolate chips/chunks (preferred) | |
