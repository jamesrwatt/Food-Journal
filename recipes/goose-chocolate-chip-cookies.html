Goose's Chocolate Chip Cookies

Oven: 350°F Bake Time: 10-12 min Shelf: To Make Dessert

Ingredients

  • 1 cup salted butter
  • 1¼ cups brown sugar
  • 1⅓ cups granulated sugar
  • 1 large egg + 2 egg yolks (room temp)
  • 1 tablespoon vanilla
  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • ¾ tsp baking soda
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 1 cup semi-sweet chocolate chips
  • 1 cup dark chocolate chips/chunks (preferred)

Instructions

  1. Whisk together flour, baking soda, and salt.
  2. In a pan over medium heat, add the butter. Cook 5-6 minutes until it bubbles, then browns. Transfer to a bowl and cool.
  3. In a large bowl, add brown sugar and granulated sugar, then pour in the butter. Mix until combined. Add the egg, egg yolks, and vanilla. Whisk until smooth.
  4. Add wet ingredients into dry and mix to form dough.
  5. Mix in chocolate chips and let dough rest 15-20 minutes.
  6. Line a sheet pan with parchment paper.
  7. Scoop dough into 1/4 to 1/3 cup portions, spaced about 6 per sheet.
  8. Bake ~10 minutes until edges are golden and centers still soft. Cookies continue baking on the pan after coming out of the oven.
